Privacy Policy

Arcloom
Last updated: September 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Arcloom ("we", "our", or "the App") collects, uses, and discloses information when you use our mobile application available on the Google Play Store.

Summary: Arcloom is an offline-first puzzle game. We do not collect personal identifying information (such as your name, email, phone number, address, or physical location). We only store your game progress locally on your device. Third-party advertising and billing services (Google AdMob and Google Play In-App Billing) may process standard device identifiers and diagnostics as described below.

1. Information We Collect

a. Information Stored Locally

All core gameplay data is stored strictly on your device using local storage:

  • Game progress (completed levels, puzzle completion times, star ratings)
  • Audio settings (sound effects and ambient music preferences)
  • Theme preferences (light, dark, AMOLED)
  • In-app purchase status (e.g. ad-free unlock flag)

This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

b. Information Collected by Third Parties

The App integrates third-party services that may collect automated technical information to provide advertising and in-app purchase functionality:

  • Google AdMob (Advertising): Google AdMob may automatically collect device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID), general IP address/coarse location, app interaction data, and diagnostic data for ad personalization, frequency capping, fraud prevention, and performance metrics. You can manage or reset your advertising ID in your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads).
  • Google Play Billing (In-App Purchases): Transactions for in-app purchases are securely processed directly by Google Play. We do not receive, process, or store your payment details, credit card numbers, or billing addresses.

2. Third-Party Service Providers

The App uses third-party services that have their own privacy policies governing data usage:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob Privacy and Terms
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

3. How We Use Information

The information processed by the App and third-party SDKs is used solely to:

  • Enable core game functionality and persist your level progress
  • Serve non-intrusive in-game advertisements via Google AdMob
  • Facilitate optional in-app purchases (e.g. ad removal) via Google Play
  • Monitor app stability, performance, and crash prevention

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share any personal user data with third parties. Any automated technical telemetry collected by Google AdMob or Google Play is handled directly by Google in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Since your game progress and settings are stored locally on your device, you have full control over your data. You can delete all locally stored game data at any time by clearing the App's data in Android Settings (Settings > Apps > Arcloom > Storage > Clear Storage/Data) or by uninstalling the App.

6. Children's Privacy

Arcloom does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13. The App is a general audience puzzle game. If you believe that a child under 13 has provided personal data through third-party services, please contact us so we can investigate and address it.

7. Security

We value your trust. We implement standard best practices to ensure your local game data is kept safe, and we rely on Google's industry-standard secure infrastructure for ad delivery and billing.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
