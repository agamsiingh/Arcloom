# Privacy Policy for Arcloom

**Last updated:** September 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how **Arcloom** ("we", "our", or "the App") collects, uses, and discloses information when you use our mobile application available on Google Play.

---

### Summary
Arcloom is an offline-first puzzle game. We do not collect or request personal identifying information (such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or precise location). Core game progress is saved strictly on your local device. Standard third-party services (Google AdMob and Google Play Billing) may process limited device telemetry for advertising and in-app purchases.

---

### 1. Information Stored Locally
All gameplay progress and user preferences are stored directly on your device via local storage:
- Completed levels, puzzle times, and star ratings
- Theme preferences (Light, Dark, AMOLED)
- Audio settings (sound effects and music volume)
- Purchase entitlement status (e.g. ad-free unlock)

This information is never transmitted to or stored on our external servers.

---

### 2. Third-Party Services & Automated Data Collection
The App integrates trusted third-party services that may automatically collect non-personal technical identifiers:
- **Google AdMob:** Delivers in-game banner and interstitial advertisements. AdMob may collect your device's Android Advertising ID, coarse IP/location, and diagnostic telemetry for ad personalization and analytics. You can reset or opt out of personalized ads via your device settings (*Settings > Google > Ads*).
- **Google Play In-App Billing:** Processes optional in-app purchases (e.g. Remove Ads). All payment transactions are handled securely by Google Play. We do not receive or store payment card or financial details.

For more details on Google's data handling practices:
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [Google AdMob Privacy](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543)
- [How Google uses data from partner apps](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads)

---

### 3. How Data Is Used
Information processed by the App or third-party SDKs is used solely to:
- Provide gameplay functionality and maintain level progression
- Display non-intrusive ads via Google AdMob
- Process optional in-app purchases via Google Play
- Ensure stability, prevent crashes, and optimize performance

---

### 4. Data Sharing
We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. Automated technical data collected by third parties is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

---

### 5. Data Retention & Deletion
Because your game data is stored locally on your device, you have complete control. You can permanently delete all local game data at any time by clearing the App's storage (*Settings > Apps > Arcloom > Storage > Clear Data*) or by uninstalling the application.

---

### 6. Children's Privacy
Arcloom does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. The application is designed for general audiences of all ages.

---

### 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be reflected with a revised "Last updated" date on this page.
